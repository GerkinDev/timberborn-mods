Gates for dams, pipes, and other water infrastructures. Warranty void if left open.

Adds 2 watertight gates variants (flat and stairs), that you can use to forbid access to your underground pipes or reservoirs. They can be automated, and has 3 opening modes:
- Open
- Closed
- Pass, that will open quickly when a beaver need to pass through (partially implemented, for now it acts as an "airlock").

Supports being placed between districts

---

This mod was originally done as a replacement to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3493733195]Luke's broken gate mod[/url], especially for the new "Pressure" map where I needed a way to control access to pipes. I hope it will serve you.
